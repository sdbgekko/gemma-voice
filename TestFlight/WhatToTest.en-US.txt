0.2.51 — STT hang fix

Fixes a case where the on-device speech-to-text could silently hang a turn: if the recognizer ended without a final result (a clean cancel or no speech detected), the app was left waiting on a callback that never came, so the turn never completed. Now it always resolves.

Please test: normal conversation should work as before. Specifically try: start speaking then go silent immediately (no words), or cancel mid-utterance — the turn should end cleanly and the mic should be ready again, never stuck.
