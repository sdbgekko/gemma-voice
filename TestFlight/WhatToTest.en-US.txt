0.2.50 — Mic reliability fixes

Two fixes under the hood:
1) The mic could occasionally go "deaf" — stop hearing you — after a phone call, Siri, or switching away mid-reply, until you restarted the app. Fixed with a watchdog that always reopens the mic.
2) A rare crash when connecting/disconnecting AirPods or headphones while talking. Fixed.

Please test: (a) talk to Gemma, trigger Siri or take a call while she's replying, come back, and confirm she still hears you ("can you hear me?"). (b) Connect/disconnect AirPods a few times mid-conversation — no crash, mic keeps working.
